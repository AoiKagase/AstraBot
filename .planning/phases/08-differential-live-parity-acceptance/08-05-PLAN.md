TeamInfo-confirmed team available to the readiness gate, and prove that the
current-source artifact reaches Nav intent generation before any live claim.

managed Bots did not act independently. Current source has two inconsistent
boundaries: `JoinController::readyEntity()` can accept an active entity without

deployed DLL is not the workspace-root DLL, so the fix must pin the rebuilt
artifact before live retesting. Do not write `entity->v.team` from TeamInfo;
retain the raw engine observation and carry an explicit confirmed-team signal.
</context>
